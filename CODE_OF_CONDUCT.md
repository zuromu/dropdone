# Code of Conduct

This is a short version based on the Contributor Covenant 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome here, no matter their age, background, identity, experience level, or how they look. We promise to keep this project friendly and harassment free.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Accept feedback without getting defensive
- Say sorry and fix it when you get something wrong

## What is not okay

- Insults, personal attacks, or trolling
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private
- Posting other people's private information
- Anything else that would be unwelcome in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and can ban people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory on this repository or message [@zuromu](https://github.com/zuromu) on GitHub. All reports are handled privately.

## Where this applies

In the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project in public.
